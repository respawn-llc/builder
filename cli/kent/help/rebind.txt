Usage of kent rebind:
  kent rebind <session-id> <new-path>

Move a session to another workspace.
Use this after moving on-disk location of the session's workspace directory or to continue a session from another workdir while keeping it attached to the same project.
`<new-path>` must exist and not be already bound to another project.

Example:
  kent rebind <session-id> ../moved-workspace
